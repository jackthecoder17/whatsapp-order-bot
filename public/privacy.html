Privacy Policy

This business WhatsApp ordering assistant · Last updated 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information our WhatsApp ordering assistant collects when you message us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, which WhatsApp shares with us when you message us.
  • The messages you send us, including your order, name and delivery address.
  • Order details such as items, totals, payment status and delivery status.

How we use it

  • To take, prepare and deliver your order.
  • To send you order confirmations and status updates on WhatsApp.
  • To answer your questions when you ask to speak with a person.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Meta / WhatsApp, which delivers messages between you and us under the WhatsApp Business terms.
  • Payment providers (for example Paystack) if you choose to pay online. They process your payment under their own privacy policies.
  • Delivery riders, who receive your name, phone number and address only to deliver your order.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for up to 12 months for accounting and customer service, then delete them. Chat messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your order.

Your choices

You can stop receiving messages at any time by not messaging us or by blocking our number on WhatsApp. To ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted, send us a WhatsApp message saying delete my data and we will do it within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message us on WhatsApp or email the business.
